Place your audio files here.

Expected files:
- ambient.mp3  → Background ambient music (plays via toggle button)
- song1.mp3    → First song in the music player
- song2.mp3    → Second song in the music player
- song3.mp3    → Third song in the music player

You can add more songs by duplicating an audio-card block in index.html.

Supported formats: .mp3, .wav, .ogg
Recommended: .mp3 for best cross-browser compatibility
